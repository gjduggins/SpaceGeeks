# NASA Missions Data Specification\n\n## Overview\nThis document defines the NASA missions data that will be included in the SpaceGeeks website. The data will be stored in the `InMemoryNasaMissionRepository` and displayed on the NASA missions page.\n\n## Mission Selection Criteria\nMissions were selected based on:\n- Historical significance\n- Scientific impact\n- Public interest\n- Educational value\n\n## Included Missions\n\n### 1. Apollo 11 (1969)\n- **Name**: Apollo 11\n- **Description**: First crewed mission to land on the Moon\n- **Launch Date**: July 16, 1969\n- **Status**: Completed\n- **Key Achievements**:\n  - First humans on the Moon\n  - Successful lunar module landing\n  - Safe return to Earth\n\n### 2. Voyager 1 (1977)\n- **Name**: Voyager 1\n- **Description**: Space probe launched to study outer Solar System and interstellar medium\n- **Launch Date**: September 5, 1977\n- **Status**: Active\n- **Key Achievements**:\n  - First spacecraft to enter interstellar space\n  - Detailed studies of Jupiter and Saturn\n  - Continues transmitting data after 40+ years\n\n### 3. Hubble Space Telescope (1990)\n- **Name**: Hubble Space Telescope\n- **Description**: Space-based optical telescope providing unprecedented views of the universe\n- **Launch Date**: April 24, 1990\n- **Status**: Active\n- **Key Achievements**:\n  - Over 1.4 million observations\n  - Determined rate of expansion of the universe\n  - Discovered dark energy\n\n### 4. Mars Rover Spirit (2004)\n- **Name**: Mars Exploration Rover Spirit\n- **Description**: Robotic rover exploring the Martian surface\n- **Launch Date**: June 10, 2003\n- **Status**: Completed (2010)\n- **Key Achievements**:\n  - Operated for over 6 years (planned 90 days)\n  - Discovered evidence of past water activity on Mars\n  - Traveled over 7.7 km\n\n### 5. Kepler Space Telescope (2009)\n- **Name**: Kepler Space Telescope\n- **Description**: Space observatory designed to discover Earth-like planets orbiting other stars\n- **Launch Date**: March 7, 2009\n- **Status**: Completed (2018)\n- **Key Achievements**:\n  - Discovered over 2,600 confirmed exoplanets\n  - Identified potentially habitable planets\n  - Revolutionized understanding of planetary systems\n\n### 6. Curiosity Rover (2012)\n- **Name**: Mars Science Laboratory Curiosity\n- **Description**: Car-sized robotic rover exploring Gale Crater on Mars\n- **Launch Date**: November 26, 2011\n- **Status**: Active\n- **Key Achievements**:\n  - Confirmed ancient Mars could support microbial life\n  - Detected organic compounds in Martian rocks\n  - Continues operations beyond planned mission duration\n\n### 7. New Horizons (2006)\n- **Name**: New Horizons\n- **Description**: Space probe launched to study Pluto and the Kuiper Belt\n- **Launch Date**: January 19, 2006\n- **Status**: Active\n- **Key Achievements**:\n  - First spacecraft to visit Pluto\n  - Revealed detailed geology of Pluto and Charon\n  - Studied Kuiper Belt object Arrokoth\n\n### 8. James Webb Space Telescope (2022)\n- **Name**: James Webb Space Telescope\n- **Description**: Large infrared telescope successor to Hubble\n- **Launch Date**: December 25, 2021\n- **Status**: Active\n- **Key Achievements**:\n  - Captured deepest infrared images of universe\n  - Detected atmospheres of distant exoplanets\n  - Observing earliest galaxies in universe\n\n### 9. Cassini-Huygens (1997)\n- **Name**: Cassini-Huygens\n- **Description**: Mission to study Saturn and its system\n- **Launch Date**: October 15, 1997\n- **Status**: Completed (2017)\n- **Key Achievements**:\n  - Discovered methane lakes on Titan\n  - Found potential habitability on Enceladus\n  - Detailed study of Saturn's rings\n\n### 10. Parker Solar Probe (2018)\n- **Name**: Parker Solar Probe\n- **Description**: Space probe designed to study the Sun's corona\n- **Launch Date**: August 12, 2018\n- **Status**: Active\n- **Key Achievements**:\n  - First spacecraft to fly through the Sun's corona\n  - Providing data on solar wind acceleration\n  - Making closest approaches to Sun in history\n\n## Data Model Properties\nEach mission will have the following properties:\n- **Name**: Official mission name\n- **Description**: Brief overview of the mission\n- **LaunchDate**: Date of launch (approximate if needed)\n- **Status**: Current mission status (Active, Completed, Planned)\n- **ImagePath**: Path to representative mission image\n- **KeyAchievements**: List of major accomplishments\n\n## Future Considerations\nAdditional missions that could be added in future updates:\n- Artemis Program\n- Europa Clipper\n- Dragonfly Mission to Titan\n- Roman Space Telescope\n
